# Ghost FTP by Brendigo — Commercial licence and legal publication review

**Legal drafting status:** EULA.txt and LICENSE.txt carry the same commercial agreement, including the current desktop distribution addendum. These are publication drafts, not evidence of review by a Croatian or EU-qualified lawyer. Preserve both distribution paths where installers, Cargo package metadata or documentation rely on them.

## Verify before accepting or distributing revised terms

- **Contracting party.** Verify the exact registered legal entity, legal form, address, contact details and contractual role of `Brendigo LTD` and `Brendigo, obrt za programiranje`. The current wording refers to more than one potential licensor; the actual seller/provider should be unambiguous in each transaction.
- **Licence and consideration.** Confirm whether the downloadable Preview is free to evaluate, paid, subscription-based or separately negotiated. Grant conditions, evaluation rights, user/device limits, termination and any activation mechanism must match the **actual** offer, not an assumed business model.
- **Consumer and business contracts.** Have counsel check Croatian and EU consumer information duties, digital-content/software conformity rights, mandatory remedies, unfair-term limits, withdrawal rules (where applicable), B2B variations and enforceability of each warranty/liability clause.
- **Privacy and security.** Verify the actual controller and contact for any Brendigo-operated endpoint, updater, support site or telemetry; lawful bases, recipients, retention, international transfers and GDPR Article 13/14 disclosures where triggered. Do not invent a blanket data-retention period.
- **Third-party intellectual property.** Resolve complete SBOM and licence obligations from the exact shipped npm, Cargo, Go and OS components. Keep attribution and any required licence text/source offers distinct from the proprietary commercial grant.
- **Acceptance and distribution.** Verify that the installer actually displays/records any required licence acceptance, that applicable terms are bundled with portable distributions, and that update flows do not impose undisclosed material changes.
- **Release scope.** Only promise Windows/Linux client functions present in a tested build. Retired Server/CLI/Demo references in historical documents do not reinstate those products. State Preview versus Stable clearly.
- **Jurisdiction, language and disputes.** Confirm the governing law, jurisdiction/venue, mandatory consumer exceptions, authoritative contract language and dispute routes with qualified counsel before legal sign-off.

**Document control:** The current addendum applies to the Ghost FTP 0.99.73 Preview source line as of 21 November 2026; the source version is not proof of a published or signed binary. Material contract changes should be approved separately and versioned with an effective date. This checklist is not legal advice or an attorney opinion.
